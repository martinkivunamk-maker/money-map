Money Map v2.0.3.23-test2 — Final Test

Changes from test1:
- Tightened the top-right menu to fit its labels cleanly.
- Removed the unnecessary divider lines between menu items.
- Replaced Android white select dialogs with Money Map’s own in-app picker.
- Limited year choices to 2020 through the current year.
- Limited the current year to the current month; future periods cannot be saved.
- Corrected the new monthly update default to the month shown in the app header.
- Added clean tap haptics to the three-dot button and all three menu actions.
- Scrolling does not trigger haptics.

Financial logic retained:
- One snapshot per item per month.
- Saving the same month replaces that month.
- Historical values are not added into net worth.
- Investment principal remains under Life Savings; only interest is counted in Assets.
