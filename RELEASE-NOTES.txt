Money Map v2.0.3.63

Upload BOTH files to the money-map-main repo, replacing what is there:
  Money_Map.html
  sw.js

sw.js carries a new cache name (moneymap-v20363-core) so the phone drops the old
copy instead of serving it from cache. Nothing else in the repo changes.

What is in this build
 1  Receipts attach again - the picker is opened inside the tap itself, so the
    Android gesture window is still alive when the gallery or camera is asked for.
 2  Remaining income - the long month-end paragraph is gone; the empty month-end
    figure in the small cards is filled, and one short line follows.
 3  Back steps out one level at a time and only closes the app from the overview.
 4  Re-opening after the idle lock lands on the overview.
 5  The welcome line now reports something real from the data, and changes daily.
 6  Side gutters tightened from 24px to 16px, WhatsApp-style.
 7  Summary cards stay put while the list under them scrolls.
 8  The financial health ring and its Good label use the same green as elsewhere.
 9  Cards flattened to pure white - the ivory tint is gone.
10  The word Details removed from every card that carried it.
11  Net-worth: centred heading, wider bars, current month in the app colour and
    older months faint.
12  Settings cards under Data, Appearance, Security and Savings explained.
13  Add category sits at the end of the list with clear ground clearance.
14  Asset history rows show the month, the amount and a pencil, plus the gain
    against the previous month.
15  Adding a goal no longer opens the keyboard before you have chosen anything.
16  Transaction day totals are no longer coloured.
17  Confirmations now appear as a bottom bar above the nav bar, in the app theme,
    with Undo where it applies.

Also in: bigger celebration button with headroom above the icon, receipt preview
zooms out far enough to crop a close-up, and receipts can be saved to the phone.

Checked before sending: every injected script block passes a syntax check, the
layout suite passes 40/40 and the behaviour suite 51/51.
