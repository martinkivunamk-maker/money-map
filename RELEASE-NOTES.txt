Money Map v2.0.3.66 - web files
Upload to money-map-main (repo root), replacing the same two files.
Files: Money_Map.html, sw.js

1. Remaining income. The closing sentence is gone completely. The month end
   figure is now built from how you actually spend: average daily outflow over
   the last 28 days with the recent fortnight weighted double, then blended
   against budget still unspent, discounted by the share of budget that past
   months show really gets spent. The old flat month-to-date average is what
   produced the 1.2M claim against a 15k balance.
2. Welcome quote may use the width of the screen instead of being squeezed
   into short balanced lines.
3. Settings keeps its scroll position. Open Data from the bottom of the list,
   come back, and you are still at the bottom. Applied to page changes too.
4. The approved bottom bar replaces the old top pill: app surface in light
   mode, dark only in dark mode, tick centred in its shaded square, UNDO on
   the right. A failed action shows a cross instead of the tick.
6. Choosing camera or gallery no longer takes the photo card down with it.
7. Welcome greeting now reports what needs attention: a category that jumped,
   the week against last week, and what this pace carries into next month.
8. Both celebration buttons match the app's own save and close buttons.
9. Asset history shows the month and the amount on one line.
10. One box titled Select the month, asking year first then month, the way
    compare periods does.
11. Financial health card face shows Loan load instead of Avg monthly surplus.

5. Back button. Page level back is improved here, but the shell installed on
   your phone (versionCode 9, 2.0.3.41) decides back on its own and never asks
   the page. Settings to overview and exit-on-overview need the new APK, whose
   source you already have as v2.0.3.65. This is not fixable from the web
   alone on the old shell.
