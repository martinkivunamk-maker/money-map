MONEY MAP - v2.0.3.60
Upload: Money_Map.html + sw.js (two files, nothing else)

What this round fixes
=====================

1. THE PROFILE SCREENS (edit picture / edit name)
   - The heading now sits below the notification bar. Both screens use the
     phone's own status-bar height, the same way every other screen in the app
     does, and the back arrow is now the same arrow, same size, same position.
   - The screens can no longer be dragged left or right, and the name screen no
     longer scrolls inside itself. Nothing on them can be wider than the screen.
   - Save now rides on top of the keyboard. It follows the keyboard up and back
     down instead of sitting underneath it.
   - Choosing camera or gallery now works. Two separate faults:
       a) the picker was being opened from a timer, so the tap had already
          expired by the time it fired - it is now opened in the same tap;
       b) the app builds its cropper inside the page, which meant it was being
          painted underneath these screens - that is why nothing appeared and
          there was nothing to crop. The cropper is now lifted to the top layer.
     A new picture also refreshes the screen you are standing on.
   - Tapping the picture buzzes once. The second buzz was mine, on top of the
     app's own.
   - The camera/gallery/remove card now opens above the enlarged picture instead
     of behind it.

2. BACK BUTTON AND BACK GESTURE - ALIGNED ACROSS THE APP
   - Only the settings screen used to leave a step behind, so back from
     transactions, analytics or budget went straight out of the app. Every screen
     change now leaves one step behind.
   - Back now walks: open layer -> previous screen -> ... -> overview -> exit.
   - The app's own settings-back is held off while one of the profile screens is
     up, which is what used to throw you out to the overview mid-gesture.
   - Exactly one owner for back now, so it behaves the same way every time.

3. FINANCIAL HEALTH (expanded)
   - Rebuilt the way you asked: one line per item, the figure and the score
     together - e.g. "Savings rate   27.8%   (24/30 points)".
   - Average month surplus comes last, marked as carrying no points.
   - The progress lines are gone and the closing note is one short line.
   - THREE different patches were writing into this card and the last timer won,
     which is why none of the changes you approved ever showed. Two of them have
     been stood down; the card now has a single owner.
   - Picking a year no longer touches the pickers at the top of the screen (that
     is what flipped the month picker to All Months). The card reads the figures
     for the year you chose without changing the app's period.
   - The year list is now a plain, readable list of the years you actually have
     entries for.

4. LOANS AND GOALS
   - The open loan card no longer repeats the balance above the three boxes.
   - A goal card no longer states what is left to go before you expand it.

Housekeeping
============
- Two dead score-builders emptied out (they were the duplicates causing item 3).
- Cache name moved to moneymap-v20360-core so phones pick the new file up.

Checked before sending

- Every edited block passes a syntax check.
- New suite L: 34 device-shaped checks (header height against the status-bar
