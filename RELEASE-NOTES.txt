Money Map v2.0.3.23 — Production

Approved production release:
- Compact top-right menu with Settings, Notification center and Recurring transactions.
- Clean tap-only haptics on the three-dot menu and its actions.
- Independent Notification center and Recurring transactions screens.
- Monthly history for physical assets, cash/accounts and investment interest.
- App-styled Month and Year pickers with right-facing arrows.
- Each item’s history begins in the month its first monthly value is recorded.
- Pickers never show periods before that starting month or after the current month.
- New months and years appear automatically as time advances.
- Same-month saves replace that month; historical values are never double-counted.
- Investment principal remains under Life Savings; only earned interest is counted in Assets.
